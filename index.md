Rental contract extracts
E1B Standard terms applicable to all 24 contracts
Lessor: Riverside Activity Center LLC. The booking ID, customer, scheduled date, and original rental charge are recorded in E1A. The original contracts are accepted by the listed customer and issued on the company’s behalf by Evan Parker. The extracts below reproduce the terms relevant to this review; signature images, addresses, and unrelated boilerplate are omitted.
Facility charge. The stated charge is for use of Riverside’s facility. It is payable to Riverside Activity Center LLC. A customer’s coaching, tournament, training, or other program arrangements do not change the lessor or the entitlement to the facility rental charge.
Payment. Full payment is normally required before the event. Credit terms require written owner approval. A payment instruction or invoice adjustment must be consistent with the company’s written authorizations.
Changes. A discount, cancellation waiving a charge, or transfer of collection rights requires written approval by Daniel Mercer. The original booking record remains part of the file after an approved change.
Separate programs. Third party coaches may conduct activities on site. Such an activity remains subject to the facility rental charge unless Daniel signs a separate written operating agreement specifying otherwise.
E1C Contract coverage and retained records
Each customer in E1A accepted a private facility rental at the listed original charge and scheduled date. The entire population uses the same lessor and standard terms above. None of these original contracts transfers the facility charge to another operator or includes a separately priced coaching service. Subsequent approvals, where present, are maintained separately from the original contract register.
For this simulation, the register and common contract extracts replace repetitive copies of 24 contracts. They establish the contractual terms for each booking ID. They do not show whether a particular event subsequently occurred or how its payment was handled.
